# Food twins: anatomy, scene graph and parameter register

Generated by `tools/anatomy_doc.py` from the built assets and `food/params.py` -- do not edit by hand.

Each food object is one USD asset whose prims under `/Food/Parts` follow the anatomical partonomy. Every prim is an SDT node: `sdt:represents` names the physical part (entity IRI), `sdt:class` its ontology class, `sdt:partOf` its parent part, `sdt:observability` how the robot can come to know it; `food:mass_g`, `food:volume_cm3`, `food:density_kgm3` come from the part's closed mesh. Beliefs live in `sdt.usda` (over `whole.usdc`), hidden instance parameters in `truth.usda`. Cuts slice every part mesh, so a half is again a complete part graph with its own mass and inertia.

Status codes: SOURCED, SOURCED-VERIFY (detail to check in the full text), STANDARD, DERIVED, TYPICAL (textbook value, no citation held), ILLUSTRATIVE (twin assumption, calibrate before quantitative claims).
