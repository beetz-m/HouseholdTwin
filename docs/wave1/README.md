## Isaac Sim benches

**Roll staling squeeze** (`scripts/sim_roll_squeeze.py`): FEM roll (crumb only) between 30 mm pads, incremental stiffness 1 -> 3 N:

| crumb E (kPa) | age | k 1000 Hz | k 2000 Hz | change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 5 h | 0.276 | 0.289 | +4.5 % |
| 70 | 48 h | 0.497 | 0.631 | +21.2 % |
| 125 | 96 h | 0.546 | 0.797 | +31.5 % |
| 157 | 168 h | 0.535 | 0.876 | +38.9 % |

k = 0.29 N/mm * E / 20 kPa: converged fresh lane (+5 % between runs) scaled linearly (small-strain elasticity, same contact geometry). Stiffer lanes under-converged (k still rising with rate) -> lower bounds only. Register: `squeeze_k_model` (DERIVED); `RollBelief.observe_stiffness`.

**Banana peeling** (`scripts/sim_banana_peel.py`): peel as strips of rigid segments with breakable adhesion / seam / hinge joints (strengths = ILLUSTRATIVE ratios x khanal23 peel fracture force); a spring finger pulls strip 1 from the snapped neck to the tip.

| stage | adhesion / seam / tear (N) | peak (N) | median (N) | strip 1 detached | other strips | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 13.9 / 9.3 / 46.4 | 14.2 | 2.67 | 11/12 | 0 | strip peeled off |
| 4 | 4.7 / 3.2 / 15.8 | 7.1 | 1.26 | 5/12 | 0 | strip torn short |
| 6 | 1.6 / 1.1 / 5.4 | 2.5 | 0.43 | 12/12 | 2 | strip peeled off |

Ripe fruit peels cleanly along the ridges at ~0.4 N median; green fruit needs ~14 N peaks and its strip may tear short (the outcome flips between runs: peeling and tearing are close). Joint forces of light segments are spiky (a stage-4 strip tore below its nominal strength). Force level inherits the ILLUSTRATIVE bond ratios: there is no published peeling force (GAP).

**Why cooks plank a carrot** (`scripts/sim_carrot_plank.py`): knife pressed up to the cutting force (44 N, jah18 scaled), straight or with a +-20 mm slicing stroke:

| carrot | knife | roll at F_cut (deg) | slide (mm) | escapes at (N) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| whole | press | 5.1 | 1.5 | - |
| whole | stroke | 26.1 | 7.2 | 1 |
| planked | press | 0.1 | 0.0 | - |
| planked | stroke | 105.1 | 389.5 | 10 |

A straight press acts through the axis of a round root and does not roll it; the slicing stroke drags the round root into rolling at once (1 N). The planked half holds up to ~10 N of press during strokes; above that the blade friction, 6 cm from its centre of mass, yaws it -- the claw-grip hand is needed.
