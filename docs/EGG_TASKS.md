# Egg kitchen tasks as action designators

Generated by `tools/egg_tasks_doc.py` from `food/egg_tasks.py`. The task knowledge was researched on cooking sites (America's Test Kitchen, egg boards, Milk Street, Food Network, USDA/CDC; wikiHow and Serious Eats only via snippets because they block automated access). Parameters marked by a twin model are computed per egg instance: strike speed by `food/egg_crack.py` (study: `tools/egg_crack_study.py`), boil time by `food/egg_cook.py` (`food.egg_tasks.best_boil_time`).

## PickUpEgg

**Goal:** egg held, shell_condition unchanged

**Parameters**

- `grip_force_N`: 1.0 (default 15.0) — H8 corridor from EggBelief; people: 'firm but gentle'
- `contact`: equator, two pads, spread contact — no point loads (ring, fingertip edge)

**Steps**

1. detect egg in carton/tray
2. check for cracks and a stuck egg (lift gently; resists -> stuck)
3. approach equator
4. close at <= 0.1 m/s
5. force-limited grip
6. lift <= 2 m/s^2

**Preconditions:** shell_condition in {intact, microcracked}; support in {carton, tray, bowl, board}

**Effects:** support := gripper

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| slip | egg lags the gripper / tilts | regrasp with higher force inside the corridor |
| crack | crack line, wet spot | shell_condition := cracked; use now, cook thoroughly |
| stuck egg | resistance on lift | free gently; cracked eggs: cook thoroughly or discard |

**Twin model:** PhysX contacts + EggTruth.static_load; EggBelief.admissible (AICOR H8)

**Sources:** usda_handling (USDA ARS egg safety handling (ars.usda.gov .../eggsafety_handlingeggs.pdf): gently remove a stuck egg; cook cracked eggs thoroughly.); masterclass_crack (MasterClass 'How to crack an egg' (snippet): hold firm but gentle.)

## TransportEgg

**Goal:** egg at target support, intact

**Parameters**

- `max_accel`: 0.5 (default 2.0) — m/s^2; keeps F_slip below grip force
- `target`: carton, bowl, cup, egg tray — never loose on a bare counter: eggs roll in curves

**Steps**

1. keep grip
2. move
3. place: descend <= 30 mm/s at contact
4. release

**Preconditions:** support = gripper

**Effects:** support := target

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| drop | egg leaves the gripper | drop impact model: towel ~10 cm safe, stone ~1 cm |

**Twin model:** EggTruth.impact (calibrated to cohen2025)

**Sources:** cookscountry_storage (Cook's Country egg storage (cookscountry.com/how_tos/5900-egg-storage): carton, middle/back shelf 35-40 F; door ~45 F.); cohen2025 (Cohen et al. 2025, Communications Physics (MIT; physics.aps.org/articles/v18/100): 180 drops onto a hard surface from 8-10 mm: side-on 5 % (8 mm) / ~45 % (10 mm) cracked, vertical 55 % / ~80 %; static breaking ~45 N in both orientations.)

## CrackEgg

**Goal:** shell cracked open, contents in the cup/bowl, yolk whole, no shell inside

**Parameters**

- `surface`: flat counter | bowl rim | another egg (default another egg) — sources disagree: atk_egg
- `strike_speed`: 0.2 (default 0.65) — m/s; twin optimum: egg-on-egg 0.5, counter 0.45, thin rim 0.25
- `into`: small cup first — catch shell and bad eggs before the batch

**Steps**

1. hold egg (PickUpEgg)
2. strike equator once
3. inspect: dent + sideways crack
4. over the cup: thumbs (or two grippers) into the dent, pull halves apart
5. inspect contents: shell fragments? yolk whole? smell

**Preconditions:** support = gripper; cup present

**Effects:** shell_condition := opened; contents := released (in cup)

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| no crack | no dent | strike again, a little faster |
| check (membrane intact) | crack but halves won't separate | tear membrane with thumb/edge |
| shattered | egg everywhere / many fragments | clean up; egg to scramble if contents saved |
| shell in cup | fragment visible | scoop with a half shell (or wet fingertip) |
| yolk broken | yolk spreads | divert egg to scrambling; keep the batch clean |

**Twin model:** food/egg_crack.py strike + open_up + fish_out

**Sources:** atk_egg (America's Test Kitchen 'Egg cracking showdown: bowl vs counter': rim tears the membrane and splits cleanly but leaves shards that can puncture the yolk; counter gives larger pieces, membrane often intact.); egginfo_crack (British Lion egginfo.co.uk 'How to crack an egg': rounded corner (bowl or pan edge), light tap; too hard on a flat surface and you get egg everywhere.); atk_egg_on_egg (America's Test Kitchen 'The best surface for cracking an egg is another egg': only the weaker egg cracks, leaving a clean divot; the membrane holds the shards.); eggs_ca (Egg Farmers of Canada (eggs.ca/?p=15718): crack on a flat surface; crack into a separate bowl first.)

## SeparateEgg

**Goal:** yolk in yolk bowl (whole), white in white bowl with no yolk

**Parameters**

- `method`: shell-to-shell | hand | slotted spoon | bottle (default slotted spoon) — shell edge can cut the yolk
- `egg_temperature`: cold — cold yolks are firmer

**Steps**

1. three bowls: transition, yolk, whites
2. crack on a flat surface (CrackEgg into transition bowl)
3. hold the yolk back (spoon/shell/hand), let the white drain
4. yolk -> yolk bowl
5. inspect white, then -> whites bowl

**Preconditions:** egg cold (fridge); three clean glass/metal bowls

**Effects:** contents := separated

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| yolk in white | yellow streak or speck | lift with a half shell; streak -> discard that white (fat stops whipping) |

**Twin model:** EC.open_up yolk-break model (age, drop height, shards); separation success ILLUSTRATIVE

**Sources:** atk_crack_separate (America's Test Kitchen 'How to crack and separate eggs' (articles/1126): cold eggs, flat surface, three bowls, half shell lifts yolk specks.); egginfo_separate (British Lion egginfo.co.uk 'How to separate egg whites': shell-to-shell, hand, bottle methods.); foodrepublic_separate (Food Republic 'Ways to separate eggs' (1769095): methods and separator tools.)

## BoilEgg

**Goal:** yolk doneness = target (runny | jammy | creamy | firm), shell intact, peels cleanly

**Parameters**

- `start`: hot (lower into simmering water) | cold (default hot) — hot start peels ~90 % vs ~50 %
- `time_min`: 4.5 (default 13.0) — food/egg_cook.py: room-temp medium egg 6 jammy, 8.5 creamy, 10+ firm; fridge egg +1-1.5 min
- `cooling`: ice bath 5 min | cold running water

**Steps**

1. bring water to a rolling simmer
2. lower egg with a spoon (no drop)
3. time
4. transfer to ice bath
5. peel: tap all over, roll, start at the blunt end under the membrane, under running water

**Preconditions:** shell_condition = intact

**Effects:** contents := cooked(doneness)

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| crack in the pot | white streams out | salt/vinegar only helps it set; lower gently next time |
| overcooked | grey-green ring (>14 min) | shorter time; cool fast |
| hard to peel | white tears | older eggs (7-10 d), hot start, peel under water |

**Twin model:** food/egg_cook.py EggHeat (conduction through the tissue map; thresholds CALIBRATED)

**Sources:** alphafoodie_boil (alphafoodie.com/how-long-to-boil-eggs: hot start, medium eggs: 4.5 min white just set/yolk very runny, 6 jammy, 7 barely runny, 8-9 set but creamy, 10-11 hard, >14 overcooked.); kenji_boil (Lopez-Alt (NYT 2019, via summaries): fridge eggs into boiling water: 6 min liquid yolk, 8.5 fudgy, 11 just firm; hot start peels cleanly ~90 % vs ~50 % cold start.); incredibleegg_boil (Incredible Egg 'How to make hard-boiled eggs': cold start, stand 9/12/15 min (M/L/XL); refrigerate eggs 7-10 d before; never microwave in shell.); milkstreet_peel (Milk Street 'How to peel hard-cooked eggs' (2020): steaming 12 min, 5 min ice bath, shake method ~60 % on warm eggs.)

## ScrambleEggs

**Goal:** curds set but glossy, no liquid egg, not browned

**Parameters**

- `heat`: low | medium | medium-high then low (default medium-high then low) — ATK dual heat
- `stir`: constant (small curds) | push edges to centre (large curds)
- `salt`: early (10-15 min before) | at the end (default early) — sources disagree

**Steps**

1. crack eggs (CrackEgg) into bowl
2. beat with fork until uniform yellow
3. butter in nonstick pan, foaming not brown
4. add eggs; scrape/push curds
5. lower heat; fold
6. off the heat while glossy

**Preconditions:** eggs opened in bowl

**Effects:** contents := scrambled

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| rubbery/weeping | tight curds in water | less heat, off earlier, add cold cream |

**Twin model:** not yet simulated (coagulation thresholds from opentextbc)

**Sources:** atk_scrambled (America's Test Kitchen 'Perfect scrambled eggs' (recipes/6604) and '6 tips' (articles/3118): fork-beat, half-and-half, medium-high then low.); incredibleegg_scrambled (Incredible Egg basic scrambled eggs: medium heat, push curds, off while glossy.); pepin_pbs (Jacques Pepin classic scrambled eggs (PBS American Masters): stop the cooking with reserved egg and cream.)

## FrySunnySideUp

**Goal:** white set and opaque incl. around the yolk, yolk whole and runny, no shell

**Parameters**

- `fat`: 1 tsp per egg
- `set_top`: lid | steam (1 tsp water + lid) | baste (default lid)
- `time_min`: 2.0 (default 4.0) — 1 min open + 2-3 min lid on low

**Steps**

1. preheat pan medium with fat
2. CrackEgg into a cup
3. slide from the cup at pan level
4. cook open ~1 min until edges white
5. lid / steam / baste until the white is opaque
6. slide out with a thin spatula

**Preconditions:** egg opened in a cup, yolk whole

**Effects:** contents := fried(sunny side up)

| failure | cue | recovery |
|---|---|---|
| broken yolk | yolk spreads | serve over hard / scramble |
| raw white near yolk | translucent ring | lid or baste longer |

**Twin model:** not yet simulated

**Sources:** loveandlemons_fried (Love & Lemons 'Fried egg': 1 tsp fat per egg, lid 2-3 min; over easy 15 s second side.); incredibleegg_fried (Incredible Egg basic fried eggs: steam-baste with 1 tsp water and a lid, or butter-baste.); kenji_baste (Lopez-Alt fried eggs via Tasting Table (1346566): baste with shimmering oil ~1 min, crisp lacy edges.)
