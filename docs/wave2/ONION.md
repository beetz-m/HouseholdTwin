# Yellow onion: research notes

The model is `food/onion.py`, the hidden-state models are in `food/onion_models.py`, and the register is `params.ONION`.

## Anatomy
The bulb is made of swollen leaf bases on a compressed stem, the basal plate, with roots underneath (`hb66_onion`).
- **Fleshy scales:** 7.6 ± 0.2 per bulb, with 2.3 growing centres (`kim_heb20`).
- **Epidermis:** the inner (adaxial) epidermis peels off as an intact film 120 µm thick (`wei01`).
- **Dry outer skins:** form from about the 6th true leaf and tear easily (`onion_misc`).
- **Lateral buds:** can make up to 1/3 of the bulb's mass.

In the twin, each scale is a revolved 2D region, and the scales converge on the basal plate.

## Numbers
- **Bulb (yellow Giza 20):** 51.7 × 57.1 mm, 78.7 g, density 1.04 g/cm³, crushing load 400–780 N, friction coefficient 0.67–1.34 (`bah04`).
- **US size class:** medium 5.1–8.3 cm.
- **Twin check:** onion_0 is 65 mm and weighs 146.5 g.
- **Stiffness:**
  - whole bulb: modulus 2.0–5.5 MPa (`jafari16`);
  - tissue: 2.2 MPa with skin, 1.4 MPa without (`wu25`);
  - epidermis: 3.5–8.0 MPa (`wei01`).
- **Tear gas:** the lachrymatory factor peaks about 1 min after cutting and is gone by about 10 min (`lokke12`). Blunt or fast knives release up to about 40× and 4× more droplets (`wu25`).
- **Storage:** 0 °C at 65–75 % relative humidity keeps 6–9 months. There is a 4–6-week rest after harvest, and curing loses 3–5 % of the weight (`hb66_onion`).
- **Kitchen:**
  - Halve through the root, peel, and keep the root end on.
  - Make 2–3 horizontal cuts, then vertical cuts, then slice across.
  - Dice sizes: mince up to 3 mm, fine 3–6, medium 6–13, coarse 13–19 mm (`onion_howto`).
  - Caramelising takes 30–45 min (TYPICAL).
